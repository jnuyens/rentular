<objective>
Alert landlords and property managers on WhatsApp (email fallback) on the day a lease's rent indexation becomes applicable, as one daily digest per person with a direct link per lease into the Rentular indexation page.

Purpose: landlords currently have to remember to open the indexation page. Belgian indexation can only be claimed going forward (a late application is money lost), so a nudge on the anniversary day is worth real money to them.

Output: users.whatsapp_number + indexation_alerts_enabled (Settings > Profile), leases.indexation_alert_sent_for dedup column, idempotent DDL file, shared indexation calculation service, indexationAlerts service + 09:00 daily worker, deep link `/indexation?lease=<id>` with login callback that keeps the query string, texts in en/nl/fr/de, unit tests.
</objective>
