<objective>
Add one-click snooze buttons to the daily indexation alert digest so a landlord who is not ready to index can push the reminder to next month, three months out, or the next yearly cycle without logging in. The digest gains an HTML version (inline-styled, table-based) with an "open indexation" button and the three snooze buttons per lease, in EN/NL/FR/DE; the plain-text body (used for WhatsApp too) carries the same links as text lines. A new signed token type, a public confirmation route, a nullable `indexation_snoozed_until` column and the matching `isAlertDue` logic make the snooze real.

Purpose: Today the digest nags once per anniversary with no way to say "not now, later". Landlords who postpone lose the reminder entirely or get no follow-up at a time they chose.
Output: schema column, snooze token lib with cross-rejection against the payment token, HTML digest, public snooze route with localized confirmation, alert selection that honours the snooze, tests, and the prod ALTER TABLE statement in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
