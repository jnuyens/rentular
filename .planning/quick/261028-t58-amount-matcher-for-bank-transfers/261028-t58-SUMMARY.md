# Quick 261028-t58: Amount matcher for bank transfers Summary

A third automatic matching pass, `autoAssignByAmount`. It assigns an unmatched incoming credit with an unknown sender IBAN to the one active lease whose pending rent equals that amount within 45 days before to 35 days after the booking date, then learns the sender IBAN on the tenant so the IBAN pass picks up later transfers.
