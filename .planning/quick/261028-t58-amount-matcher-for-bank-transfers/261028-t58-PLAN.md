<objective>
Add a third automatic matching pass for incoming bank transfers: when an unmatched credit carries no structured communication and comes from an IBAN nobody knows yet, assign it by unique amount to the one active lease whose pending rent matches, then remember the sender's IBAN on the tenant so the existing IBAN pass (pass 2) handles the next transfer.

Purpose: Real case Zaitsev. Rent 499, pending row due 2026-11-01, paid 499 on 2026-10-27 from an unknown IBAN with no OGM. It stayed unmatched and needed manual work. The same owner also has many unmatched transfers from his own accounts (NUYENS JASPER, LINUX BELGIUM BV); those must never be matched to rent.

Output: `apps/api/src/services/amountMatcher.ts` exporting `autoAssignByAmount`, wired at both existing call sites, with a vitest suite in the existing ibanMatcher mocking style.
</objective>
