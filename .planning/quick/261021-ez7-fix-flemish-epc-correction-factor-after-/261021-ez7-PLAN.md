Official simplified correction factors (vlaanderen.be page above). Anniversary month = month of the lease START date. Columns: D / E, F or no EPC.

| Month | d | efNone |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January | 0.951950895 | 0.903901791 |
| 2 February | 0.961757813 | 0.923515625 |
| 3 March | 0.967996216 | 0.935992433 |
| 4 April | 0.965766823 | 0.931533646 |
| 5 May | 0.971941594 | 0.943883189 |
| 6 June | 0.972124068 | 0.944248135 |
| 7 July | 0.976119286 | 0.952238571 |
| 8 August | 0.977109655 | 0.954219311 |
| 9 September | 0.980049682 | 0.960099363 |
| 10 October | 0.949447646 | 0.898895293 |
| 11 November | 0.945356473 | 0.890712946 |
| 12 December | 0.951977401 | 0.903954802 |

Label mapping: A++, A+, A, B, C: no factor (full indexation). D: `d` column. E, F, null/unknown ("none"): `efNone` column. G (not on the page, worse than F, already flagged in FLANDERS_EPC_NEEDS_CORRECTION): `efNone` column (executor discretion, documented in a comment).
Official example: base 600.00, 132.96 / 108.98 -> 732.02; EPC D, anniversary November -> 732.02 x 0.945356473 = 692.02.
