<objective>
Let the landlord choose from which rent due date an indexation takes effect, instead of always the next due date after today.

Purpose: Belgian landlords may index with up to 3 months of back-indexation (residential) or back to the anniversary (commercial). Today the app only offers the next due date, so a landlord who applies a little late loses months of indexed rent the law allows.

Output: a pure, TZ-independent options helper in indexationCalc.ts with tests; `effectiveDate` accepted and validated on the preview and apply endpoints and propagated to the letter, the indexation_records row and the response; `lastIndexationDate` stored as the legal anniversary date; a "New rent from" select in the web apply dialog with a backdating note, in four languages.
</objective>
